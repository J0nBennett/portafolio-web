AI has moved from a curiosity to a major productivity tool in web development. Beyond code completion, it is changing how interfaces are conceived and built.

## The Shift from Writing to Curation
A developer's value was once closely tied to writing complex syntax correctly. With tools such as **GitHub Copilot** and **Cursor**, more attention shifts towards **technical curation**: expressing requirements clearly, assessing code security and performance, and connecting architectural components.

## Tools Shaping the 2025 Discussion
### 1. Generative UI: v0, Bolt.new and Lovable
Generative platforms can turn a description of a component or page into a functional starting point using React, Tailwind and animations.

### 2. Assisted Large-scale Refactoring
Models such as Claude 3.5 Sonnet and GPT-4o were discussed for their ability to interpret codebases and assist tasks such as state-library migrations or legacy-component refactoring. AI agents can help coordinate this work.

## The Near Future: Intelligent Low-code Development
AI raises the bar for reviewing and integrating software. Natural-language development tools can free developers to focus on user experience, accessibility and complex business logic.

## Conclusion
The practical goal is to integrate AI as a tool that extends engineering capabilities, while retaining human judgement over the result.
