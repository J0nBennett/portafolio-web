AI allows websites to adapt to behaviour, context and intent, moving beyond a single interface for every visitor.

## From Segmentation to Individualisation
Traditional approaches group users into personas such as students or professionals. Predictive models can instead analyse clicks, time spent and navigation patterns to estimate what an individual may need next.

## Implementations Discussed in 2025
### 1. Adaptive Layouts
Websites can reorganise sections based on usage history. A visitor who frequently opens documentation might see that section more prominently.

### 2. Dynamic Copy
Generative AI can adapt tone and messaging to the visitor's context. The same product may be described technically for a developer or in terms of outcomes for a manager.

### 3. Semantic Search
Concept-based search can go beyond matching keywords. A request for something relaxing may lead to ambient music or candles rather than only items containing that word.

## The Role of Edge Data Processing
Personalisation must avoid introducing noticeable delays. Running lightweight models at the **edge** can reduce round trips to central servers and support responsive adaptation.

## Ethics and Privacy
Balancing personalisation with privacy remains essential. Clear information about data use and protection helps maintain trust.

## Conclusion
AI-driven personalisation can make experiences more relevant when it is implemented with attention to responsiveness, transparency and user privacy.
