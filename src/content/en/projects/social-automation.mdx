## The Project
Automating IBM Joven's digital presence so that content can flow between social platforms without constant manual intervention.

## Implementation
Using n8n as the central workflow engine, I designed flows to capture updates, process multimedia content and distribute scheduled posts to Instagram, Twitter and other channels.

## Benefits
- Less time spent on weekly social media management.
- More consistent branding and posting frequency.
- Automated reporting on post reach and engagement.
